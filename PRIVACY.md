# Privacy Policy — Prompt Stash

_Last updated: 2026-06-04_

**Prompt Stash does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.**

- **Your prompts stay on your device.** Everything you save (titles, prompt text, folders) is stored
  locally in your browser using `chrome.storage.local`. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else.
- **No tracking, no analytics, no accounts.** We do not collect personal information, browsing
  history, or usage data.
- **License verification (Pro only).** If you choose to enter a Pro license key, the extension sends
  only that key to Gumroad's license-verification API to confirm it is valid. No personal or browsing
  data is sent. This happens only when you click "Activate."

We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, do not use it for any purpose unrelated to the
extension's single purpose, and do not use it for creditworthiness or lending.

**Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/devloadout/prompt-stash or reply to your Gumroad receipt.
